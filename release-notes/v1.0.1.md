A bug-fix update for MediaForge 1.0.

## Fixes

- **No more "Unexpected error" pop-up when toasts close.** Clicking a message at the bottom of the window, or a fourth message pushing the oldest one out, could show "Internal C++ object (_Toast) already deleted". Nothing was ever lost, and the error no longer happens.
- **Ctrl+Q quits MediaForge on Windows.** The shortcut didn't do anything on Windows before.
- **Messages point to the right place.** Two messages told you to look in "File > Settings", a menu that doesn't exist. They now say Settings.

## Updating

If you have 1.0.0 installed, MediaForge offers this update by itself (or choose **Updates** in the sidebar). Otherwise download **MediaForge_setup_v1.0.1.exe** below and run it; it upgrades your current install.
