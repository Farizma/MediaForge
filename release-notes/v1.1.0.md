MediaForge 1.1 adds drag and drop everywhere.

## New

- **Drop files anywhere.** Drag video or audio files, or whole folders, onto the window. On Convert or Join they go to that page; from anywhere else, drop on the Convert or Join half. Folders add the media files inside them.
- **Drop links to download.** Drag a link, or a browser tab's address, onto MediaForge and it lands in the Download page's link box.
- **Reorder the queue.** Drag waiting or paused jobs to change what runs next, or select one and press Alt+Up / Alt+Down. The order is kept after a restart.
- **Open with MediaForge.** Right-click a video or audio file in Explorer and choose *Open with > MediaForge*, or drop files on the MediaForge icon, to open them in Convert.
- **One window.** Opening files while MediaForge is already running sends them to the open window instead of starting a second copy.

## Updating

If you have MediaForge 1.0 installed, it offers this update by itself (or choose **Updates** in the sidebar). Otherwise download **MediaForge_setup_v1.1.0.exe** below and run it.
